  Improved failure recovery and stop and restart cycle time.

- **AspenTech inmation Interface - Trend Resolution Factor:**  
  Added a Trend Resolution Factor setting to tune trend density.

**Compatibility:**  
Tested with inmation version v1.78.4.13668.
